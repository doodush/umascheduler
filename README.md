Ever wanted to get the Completionist title for your favourite Uma? This tool can help make the process a much easier one!

## What is UmaScheduler?
UmaScheduler is a management tool that tracks your completionist progress along with an in-built scheduler to plan multiple careers in advance.

## Why use UmaScheduler?
When I began my first completionist, I noticed it was annoying to constantly track races I haven't ran in the Trophy Room and it was hard figuring out how to schedule races efficiently based on the races left. Therefore, I started working on this tool which made the whole process much simpler. Since then, I've gotten 26 completionist titles and I'm aiming to get one for all my Umas.

## How to use UmaScheduler?
To track your completionist progress, head to the Trophy Room in-game and filter by the specific Uma. Then, check the completed races in the Checklist.

The scheduler automatically schedules races, which can be filtered by aptitudes or tracks. You can also manually replace or remove races. There is also an experimental feature to limit max consecutive races. After the career, you can mark races in the current plan as completed with 'Complete schedule'.

The plans feature allow you to plan future schedules so you're able to see what's left after each plan.

At the top, it displays the completionist progress by grade (G1,G2,G3) and breaks down remaining races by aptitude and track.

If you want to share or transfer the checklist and plans between devices, there is the option to import/export data.
